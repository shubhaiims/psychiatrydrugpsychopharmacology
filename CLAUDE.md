# Psychiatry Made Easy: content rules

These rules apply to all drug and adverse-effect content in this repository.

## Writing clinical content
- No country-wise sections, and no mention of a drug's availability or approval in particular countries. Readers worldwide use the site, and clinicians decide for themselves (the site disclaimer covers clinical judgement).
- Keep pages simple and uncluttered. Short bullets, tables for comparisons, no repeated facts.
- Obesity and weight-loss medication lists use FDA-approved drugs only.
- No brand names. Use generic names only.
- Cite with numbered references in the text, e.g. [1] or [2,3], and give one numbered reference list at the bottom of each adverse-effect topic or drug page.
- Every number needs a source. Anything not yet checked is marked [verify] until the user confirms it.

## Sources the user has chosen
- Antipsychotic weight-gain ratings: The Maudsley Prescribing Guidelines in Psychiatry, 15th ed. (Taylor, Barnes, Young), Table 1.31.
- Other drug classes' weight-gain ratings: Kaplan & Sadock's Comprehensive Textbook of Psychiatry, 11th ed., Table 27.4-5.
- Approach to antipsychotic-induced weight gain and metformin: Carolan et al., Schizophr Bull 2025;51(5):1193-1205.
- Obesity medications, goals and follow-up: ADA Standards of Care in Overweight and Obesity, Diabetes Obes Cardiometab CARE 2026;1:5-36.

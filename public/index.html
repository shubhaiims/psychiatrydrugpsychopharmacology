Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. Psychiatry Made Easy is an educational reference. Verify all drug information against current prescribing information, local protocols, and clinical judgment.

Who is it for?

Medical students, psychiatry trainees and clinicians who want quick, structured answers about psychiatric medicines.

What can I use without an account?

The calculators and the drug library browser are open to everyone. Full monographs and Ask My Notes need a free account.

Will more drugs be added?

Yes. New monographs are added over time, and the numbers on this page update as they are.

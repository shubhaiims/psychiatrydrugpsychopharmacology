Refunds and cancellation.

This page explains how cancelling and refunds work, and what to do if a payment goes wrong.

How membership works

Membership is a one-time payment for a set number of days of access, shown on the Membership page. It does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel. Your access ends on the date shown on your Account page unless you pay again.

If a payment goes wrong

  • Money was taken but your membership is not active. Finished payments are applied automatically. Wait a minute, then refresh the Membership page. If it is still not active, email us with the payment ID and we will look into it.
  • You paid twice by mistake. Each payment adds more days to your membership. Contact us within [REFUND WINDOW, for example 7 days] of paying and we will review it.
  • The payment failed but money left your account. The amount is normally returned automatically. If it has not come back after [NUMBER OF DAYS, for example 7] working days, contact us.
